ABOUT / APPROACH

Building at the intersection of technology and operations.

Ghifarion is an independent portfolio documenting practical work across data operations, software, automation, and applied AI.

The work begins with operational problems: repetitive tasks, fragmented workflows, quality checks, and the systems people rely on every day. From there, I explore how software and AI can make those processes clearer and more useful.

Professional background

My experience includes data annotation and validation workflows, quality checks, structured data processing, team coordination, workflow improvement, visual communication, and collaborative projects. These experiences shaped an interest in connecting people, processes, and technology.

How I think

  • Problem first — understand the underlying need before choosing a tool.
  • Systems over isolated tasks — consider the full workflow and its dependencies.
  • Build through experimentation — prototype, test assumptions, learn, and improve.
  • Technology with purpose — prioritize usefulness, responsibility, and meaningful outcomes.

Current exploration

AI engineering foundations, LLM integrations, AI agents and MCP, software architecture, workflow automation, data quality management, and technology leadership.

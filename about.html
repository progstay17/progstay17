ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

One identity. A practice built across technology and operations.

I am Fadhil Ghifarion. Ghifarion is the personal and professional identity I use, while 法迪 is my Chinese name. They are not separate people, brands, or projects — they all refer to me.

This portfolio brings together my work, experiments, and ongoing learning under one identity: Ghifarion — 法迪. The name may appear in different forms, but the person, perspective, and body of work are the same.

What I work on

My work moves across data operations, software, practical automation, and applied AI. I am interested in how technology can solve real operational problems: repetitive tasks, fragmented workflows, quality checks, and the systems people rely on every day.

Professional background

My experience includes data annotation and validation workflows, quality checks, structured data processing, team coordination, workflow improvement, and collaborative projects. These experiences shaped my interest in connecting people, processes, and technology.

How I think

  • Build through experimentation — prototype, test assumptions, learn, and improve.
  • Technology with purpose — prioritize usefulness, responsibility, and meaningful outcomes.

Warnafusi

Warnafusi is a colour-based board game framework designed to explore interdisciplinary education through colour combinations, customizable challenge cards, gamification, and social interaction.

The game is designed for 2–4 players, with an estimated play time of 30–60 minutes. Its learning themes include science, mathematics, and history.

Research focus

  • Colour combinations as a basis for game interaction
  • Customizable challenge cards
  • Gamification and social interaction
  • Interdisciplinary learning across science, mathematics, and history

Academic publication

Warnafusi: A Colour-Based Board Game Framework for Interdisciplinary Education. Indo-MathEdu Intellectuals Journal. Published June 1, 2025.

This research is presented separately from the software project portfolio.
